Privacy Policy

The developer "koshikun" (the "Developer") establishes this Privacy Policy (this "Policy") regarding the handling of user information in Chinchiro Monster (the "App") as follows.

Article 1 (Information Collected)

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information, such as the user's name or email address.

The information the App stores on the device is as follows. None of it is sent by the App to any external server, and all of it is excluded from Android's backup feature.

  • Sound effect ON/OFF setting
  • Username (optional, up to 8 characters. Displayed on the screens of the other users in the same room when the App's feature for playing against nearby opponents is used. See Article 2 for details)
  • Information used by the App's feature for playing against nearby opponents (a randomly generated player ID created by the App, the character the user selected, the room password the user entered, and whether the user was the host or a guest. Used only to start, resume, and verify a match. See Article 2 for details)

In addition, the Google AdMob SDK used by the App for ad delivery stores information needed to control ad display on the device, and collects information such as the Advertising ID (see Article 3 for details).

Article 2 (Transmission and Third-Party Disclosure of Information)

The App's feature for finding and playing against nearby opponents communicates directly between devices via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi and does not go through the internet (an external server). While this feature is in use, the information exchanged with nearby devices consists of the username, the selected character, the room password, the random player ID described above, and the in-game information needed to play the match (such as dice results, turn order, and character health). None of this is sent to any server operated by the Developer or a third party.

When creating a room, the App broadcasts an identifier derived from the password, together with the character selected by the user who created the room, to nearby devices. Because of how short-range communication works, this broadcast information may also reach nearby devices that are not taking part in the match.

Depending on the Android version, the OS may request permission for location, or for access to the local network (communication with devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network), because of how this short-range communication works. These are used solely to find and communicate with the devices of nearby opponents; the App does not itself collect, store, or transmit location data, nor does it collect information about devices on the local network or send such information anywhere.

The App connects to the internet only for the ad delivery described in Article 3. Except for information provided to the ad delivery service, the App does not send the information it collects to any external server or disclose it to any third party.

Article 3 (Advertising and Analytics Tools)

The App displays advertisements (full-screen ads) using "Google AdMob," an ad delivery service provided by Google Japan G.K. An ad may be shown when the user finishes by selecting "Quit" on the match result screen or from the in-match menu, or "Leave" in a room for playing against nearby opponents. Ads are not shown every time: one may be shown once these actions reach a certain count, or once a certain amount of time has passed since the previous ad.

For ad delivery, AdMob may collect information such as the Advertising ID, the IP address, device information, and information about the display of and interaction with ads. For details on how this information is handled, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy ( https://policies.google.com/privacy ).

The Developer currently does not use any analytics tools of its own. Measurement associated with ad delivery is governed by Google's Privacy Policy referenced above.

Article 4 (Use by Children)

The contents of this Policy apply equally to all users, including children. The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as a name or email address; however, as described in Article 3, information such as the Advertising ID is collected for ad delivery. The App is not offered as part of the Google Play Families program.

Article 5 (Changes to this Policy)

The contents of this Policy may be changed without prior notice as necessary. The revised contents shall take effect once displayed within the App.
